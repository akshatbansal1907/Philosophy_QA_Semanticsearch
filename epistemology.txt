Epistemology is the philosophical study of knowledge, belief, justification, and related questions about how people know what they claim to know. It asks questions such as what counts as knowledge, whether certainty is possible, how evidence supports beliefs, and what separates justified belief from mere opinion. These questions connect philosophy with reasoning, perception, memory, testimony, and skepticism.

A traditional analysis describes knowledge using concepts such as belief and justification, while later philosophers have challenged whether these conditions are sufficient in every case. Epistemology also examines sources of knowledge and the limits of human understanding. For example, a philosopher may investigate whether perception is reliable, how testimony can provide knowledge, or whether a person can be justified when evidence is incomplete.
