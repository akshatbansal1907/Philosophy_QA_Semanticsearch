Existentialism is a philosophical tradition that emphasizes individual existence, freedom, choice, responsibility, and the search for meaning. Existentialist thinkers often begin with the concrete experience of a person living in a world that does not automatically provide a single, universally accepted purpose. Human beings therefore confront questions about how they should live and what values they should adopt.

A central theme of existentialism is freedom. Because people make choices, they also have responsibility for those choices. Existentialist discussions of authenticity ask people to recognize their freedom and live according to values they genuinely take responsibility for, rather than simply following social expectations. The tradition includes different thinkers and positions, so existentialism is not one single doctrine.
