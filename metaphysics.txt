Metaphysics is the branch of philosophy that investigates fundamental questions about reality, existence, being, identity, causation, possibility, and time. Metaphysical questions may ask what kinds of things exist, what it means for something to be real, or how objects remain the same through change. Different philosophical traditions develop different answers to these questions.

Some metaphysical discussions distinguish between appearance and reality, while others examine whether reality is fundamentally composed of physical substances, minds, events, properties, or other kinds of entities. Metaphysics also considers questions about causation, necessity, possibility, and personal identity. Its goal is not simply to describe particular observations but to examine the underlying concepts and assumptions used when people think about reality.
