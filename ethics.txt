Ethics is the branch of philosophy concerned with questions about how people ought to act and what makes actions, intentions, or ways of living good or right. Ethical theories can examine consequences, duties, virtues, character, fairness, and relationships. These approaches can give different answers to the same moral problem because they emphasize different principles.

Virtue ethics focuses especially on character and the development of good habits. Instead of asking only whether one individual action follows a rule, virtue ethics asks what a virtuous person would do and what qualities contribute to a good human life. Important virtues may include courage, justice, honesty, and practical wisdom, although particular philosophical traditions understand these virtues in different ways.
