Stoicism is an ancient philosophical tradition associated with thinkers such as Zeno of Citium, Seneca, Epictetus, and Marcus Aurelius. Stoic philosophy emphasizes living rationally and developing a virtuous character. A well-known Stoic idea is that people should distinguish between what is within their control and what is not within their control.

Stoic thinkers argue that judgments and responses are more directly under our control than external events. This does not mean that external events are irrelevant, but it encourages a person to focus effort on their own choices, attitudes, and actions. Stoicism therefore connects self-discipline, reason, resilience, and virtue with the pursuit of a good life.
